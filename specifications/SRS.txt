Interior Design Recommendator SRS
Software Requirements Specification | Version 2.0 | 9 October 2026
1 Introduction
Purpose
A 2,000+ product furniture store provides interior AI advice and separate support chat. Customers submit room photos/preferences, receive explained catalogue recommendations, preview exact variants and choose whether to buy.
Scope
Scope: catalogue/variants, filters, accounts, wishlist/cart, confirmed orders/history, private designs, recommendations, single-item previews, shared quotas and admin. Catalogue changes update shopping and AI retrieval. Prototype uses labeled demo orders/local overlays; real rendering, payments, notifications and tickets need integrations. Future: multi-item previews, mobile apps, subscriptions, AR and 3D.
Objectives
Objectives: grounded advice, explained suitability, budget/size checks, private room content, AI cost control and normalized transactional data.
2 Overall Description
Product overview
Technology: TypeScript, React, Vite and Tailwind CSS; Supabase PostgreSQL, Auth, Storage and Edge Functions. Server functions enforce permissions, prices, quotas and AI calls; persistent jobs and a configured processor support asynchronous previews. Product media is public; room assets are private.
Main users
User
Responsibilities and access
Guest
Browse and search; obtain design advice; use general support chat; request previews within the guest allowance. Sign in to save designs or checkout.
Customer
Manage their own profile, wishlist, cart, orders and saved designs; use the daily preview allowance; delete their room content.
Administrator
Maintain products, variants, stock and policies; manage orders and permitted account actions; configure AI limits; review failures and audit records.
AI roles and operating assumptions
Interior AI ranks eligible furniture and explains its effect; support answers approved shopping/owned-order queries. AI cannot authorize, order or verify payment. Retailers supply accurate data/policies; customers provide measurements, which ordinary photos cannot prove. PKR currency; Asia/Karachi reset; English with translation-ready text. Credentials/capacity are deployment dependencies.
Acceptance checks
Verify updates/archives, empty preferences/no matches, missing measurements, hard budgets/size/orientation, concurrent quota reservations, refunds/releases, reset timing, duplicate jobs/orders/payment events, guest claims, unauthorized access, expiry/deletion/late results, price changes, stock races, outage recovery, truthful demo labels, private saving, isolated restoration, accessibility and the 20-room image evaluation.

4 Non Functional Requirements
ID
Category
Verification target
NFR-01
Capacity and speed
At least 2,000 products plus variants and 24 cards per page. Prototype targets: catalogue API p95 within 2 seconds with 20 concurrent users; preview acceptance within 3 seconds excluding upload. Measure external generation latency separately.
NFR-02
Security
Use HTTPS, validated inputs, parameterized access, server-only secrets, ownership checks and restricted roles. Public admin access must require MFA. Unauthorized order, asset, design and job reads must fail.
NFR-03
Privacy and uploads
Keep room files private with limited access and separate consent. Accept JPEG, PNG or WebP up to 10 MB and 20 megapixels per photo; reject corrupt files before provider calls.
NFR-04
Reliability
Persist jobs; use bounded retries and a configured processor. Provider outages must leave catalogue shopping usable. Duplicate requests and execution must not duplicate orders, stock deductions or credit finalization.
NFR-05
Usability
Provide responsive layouts, keyboard controls, labeled forms, readable contrast and announced errors and job status. Target WCAG 2.2 AA for the main browsing, design and checkout flows.
NFR-06
AI quality
Validate every recommended variant and display an explicit fit status. Evaluate enabled image generation on 20 permitted room images for variant fidelity, room preservation and placement before public use.
NFR-07
Maintenance and recovery
Use shared input schemas, versioned SQL migrations and configurable provider adapters. Document database and media recovery; demonstrate restoration of sample products and orders in an isolated environment.
NFR-08
Cost control
Stay within configured free capacity with a global cap and pause switch. Unavailable services must show fallback behavior and reject new AI jobs without credits or an automatic paid fallback.
5 Database Requirements
Data area
Main entities and essential records
Identity
AuthUser, Profile, GuestSession, AISubject. Identity, roles, active status and one verified account or guest owner per AI subject.
Catalogue
Category, Product, ProductVariant, ProductMedia, Style. Unique SKU, attributes, dimensions, price, currency, stock and media for each variant.
Shopping
WishlistItem, Cart, CartItem, Order, OrderItem. Selected variants, quantities, confirmed totals and immutable purchase snapshots.
Stock and payment
InventoryReservation, InventoryAdjustment, Payment, PaymentEvent. Once-only stock changes; independent payment state and deduplicated verified events.
Room designs
RoomSession, RoomAsset, RecommendationRun, RecommendationItem, SavedDesign. Preferences, private files, current variant links, vibe text, fit status and saved ownership.
Preview quota
PreviewJob, PreviewResult, QuotaWindow, QuotaEntry. Job state, request key and one reserved, consumed or released credit entry per real AI job.
Knowledge and operations
PolicyVersion, SearchUpdateEvent, Setting, AuditLog, ConsentRecord, CleanupTask. Approved knowledge, settings, audits, consent, update retries and asset deletion work.
Use primary/foreign keys, 3NF, unique SKUs/request keys, valid-state checks, quantities >0 and nonnegative stock. Money uses integer minor units; dimensions use decimal centimeters. Transactions protect checkout/quota; indexes support search/ownership. Category–Product, Product–Variant and RoomSession–Asset are one-to-many; OrderItem links orders/variants. Archive products while preserving historical orders.
6 Business Rules
ID
Rule
BR-01
Only active existing variants with enough stock; live database facts control identity, materials, price and availability.
BR-02
One real-preview allowance; text, support, samples and local overlays use no image credits.
BR-03
Guests: three total previews per validated identity, then sign-up; abuse limits/global cap also apply.
BR-04
Accounts: configurable, initially ten/day when capacity permits; midnight Asia/Karachi reset shown exactly. No guest-credit transfer.
BR-05
Reserve once on acceptance; consume once on success; release once on failure/cancel/expiry. Exclude reservations from availability; retain original window.
BR-06
Duplicate keys reuse job/order; regeneration needs new key. Reconcile uncertain provider outcomes before resubmission.
BR-07
Fit uses supplied placement bounds/permitted orientation; missing measurements mean unknown. Room size/images cannot prove passage clearance.
BR-08
Live price × quantity meets hard budget; sum whole-selection budgets. Never silently relax budget, size or availability.
BR-09
Delete unsaved sources after terminal processing; abandoned sources/unsaved previews expire within 24 hours. Saved assets private; block expired access/retry cleanup.
BR-10
Explicit checkout; once-only stock; immutable snapshots; payment separate. Verify provider events or authorized COD; AI/redirects prove nothing; demo collects no card data.
BR-11
Preview is illustrative; original product photos/dimensions remain purchase reference. Marketing needs separate revocable opt-in.
7 Use Cases
Use case
Main flow and exceptions
UC-01 Register/sign in
Guest/customer completes a configured identity flow; invalid credentials fail. Account allowance starts after sign-in. Transfer guest room content only through a verified claim.
UC-02 Obtain recommendations
Guest/customer enters room/preferences. Backend validates constraints and ranking and returns current variants, vibe and fit notes. Missing measurements show unknown; no matches invite revised constraints.
UC-03 Preview
Owner with a base photo selects Try This Item. Check variant, mode and quota; reserve a credit and queue. Success consumes, failure releases, duplicate requests reuse. Disabled rendering offers a labeled local overlay.
UC-04 Limit reached
Reject new AI previews without reservation. Guests see sign-up; customers see daily reset. Shopping/text advice remain available under separate rate limits.
UC-05 Save/delete
Customer explicitly saves owned unexpired assets privately. Delete blocks access, cancels jobs and removes original/derived/late outputs. Guests sign in to save; expired assets cannot be restored by saving.
UC-06 Support
Approved retrieval answers product/policy/site questions with links. Order queries require ownership. Unknown answers route to configured support; design questions route to interior advice.
UC-07 Confirm order
Recheck cart totals/stock; customer confirms delivery and amount. A transaction creates one order and allocation. Duplicates return that order; conflicts require review. Payment awaits verification; test checkout is labeled demo.
UC-08 Admin maintenance
Preview edits/CSV, apply valid records, and commit database, audit and search events. Processor refreshes retrieval/retries failures. Admins publish policy, change limits or pause previews; validation errors explain rejected changes.
8 Conclusion
Grounded advice and illustrative previews support shopping; separate AI roles, quotas, private assets and transactions protect ownership and purchase history.
